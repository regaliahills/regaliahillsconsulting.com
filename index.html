        How it works

        The discovery review, step by step

        Three conversations and a review of your books. It costs nothing, and the written findings are yours to keep whether or not you hire us.

        1. 130 minutes

          First conversation

          We learn the business, your goals, and what isn't working. If it looks like a fit, we explain the review and ask for access to your books.

        2. 2About 2 days

          QuickBooks review

          You give us accountant access to your QuickBooks. We pull your financial statements and scan the ledger for errors, sized in dollars where we can.

        3. 360 minutes

          Priorities interview

          Your priorities, roadblocks, and reporting needs, and the systems you use. We also confirm any findings that need your documents.

        4. 445 minutes

          Findings and proposal

          We walk you through what we found, show your own numbers in our reporting dashboard, and give you a proposal with a defined scope and a flat monthly fee.

        If you hire us · first 30 to 60 days

        Setup, and cleanup if needed

        We set up your workflows and reporting. If the review shows your books need it, we also do a one-time cleanup. Not every client needs one. You know the scope and cost before we start.

        Every month after

        A reviewed close

        Books closed and reviewed, your dashboard updated, and review calls based on your service level.

        Questions

        What owners ask first

        Is the discovery review really free?

        Yes. We review your books and give you a proposal before you commit to anything. The written findings are yours to keep whether or not you hire us.

        What happens after the review?

        If you hire us, we set up your workflows and reporting, and do a one-time cleanup if the review shows your books need one. After that you get a reviewed monthly close, an updated dashboard, and review calls based on your service level.

        What does CFO-level advisory include?

        Cash flow projections, margin analysis, budgets and forecasts, and help thinking through big decisions. Beyond that, we advise in whatever areas your business needs most.

        Can't AI just do my accounting, or be my CFO?

        AI is a big part of how we work, and it is very good at the repetitive parts. On its own, though, it answers only the question you ask, it can be confidently wrong, and it is only as good as the books it reads. It takes someone who speaks accounting and finance to know what to ask, to spot an answer that can't be right, and to stand behind the numbers. With us you get both: people with years of hands-on experience, including a licensed CPA, who have built their work around these tools. Read our short guide.

        How do you use AI and automation?

        They handle the repetitive work and review your books line by line, which means fewer errors, faster closes, and more of our time on your questions. We use a secure AI provider through a business account, under terms that do not use your data to train its models. A person still reviews every monthly close.

        Can you update the systems we use for bills and invoicing?

        Yes. We've moved clients' bill pay, payroll, and invoicing onto modern tools. If the review shows your systems are slowing you down, we scope that into the proposal.

        I already have a bookkeeper. Do I have to replace them?

        No. At the oversight and advisory level, your bookkeeper keeps the books and we review them each month and advise you.

        Do you work with my tax preparer?

        Yes. Your tax preparer gets organized, reconciled records at year-end, so there's no scramble or cleanup bill.

        How is it priced?

        A flat monthly fee for your service level, set after the review. If the books need a one-time cleanup, you get the scope and cost for that before we start.

        What if I'm not on QuickBooks Online?

        Send us exported reports from your accounting system and we work from those.
